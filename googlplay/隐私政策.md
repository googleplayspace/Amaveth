# Amaveth Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

## Who we are

Amaveth is operated by AFRICAN PROVERBS PAGE LLC. For privacy questions, contact raincofarri@gmail.com.

## Information used by the app

The personal finance workspace lets you enter amounts, dates, categories, account names, opening balances, notes, budgets, and currency rates. A finance account is a local bookkeeping label and does not connect to a bank. These records are stored in an encrypted database on your device. The database key is held through the operating system's secure storage. The app does not require registration to use this workspace, and does not initiate payments or access bank credentials.

If a published version provides online messaging, the messaging service processes the account details you submit, messages, contacts you add in the service, group participation, and media you choose to send. This online data is separate from your device's local finance records. Online features require a network connection. Access to online features may vary by the app's configuration; the finance workspace does not require a messaging account.

## Device permissions and notifications

When an online feature needs the camera, microphone, photo picker, or location, the app requests the relevant permission. You can refuse or revoke permission through device settings, although the corresponding feature may be unavailable. The app does not need these permissions to record local expenses. Selecting a contact in the messaging service is not a claim that the app reads your device address book.

When push notifications are configured and enabled, OneSignal and the platform push provider process a push token, subscription identifier, device and delivery information, and an account identifier used to route notifications. Notification content and associated navigation data may be processed to display a notification or open its destination. You can manage notifications in device settings. A version built without online and push features does not register for those features.

## How information is used and shared

Information is used to provide the feature you request, save and display your records, operate messaging where available, deliver enabled notifications, and diagnose service failures. Local finance records are not automatically uploaded to a finance server by this app. When you export a CSV file, you choose its destination and are responsible for sharing it securely. The app does not offer advertising or sell your local finance records.

Online features communicate with the configured service and its infrastructure providers. The app may open external websites or a third-party app at your request. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Push services include [OneSignal](https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy) and [Google Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Local finance records remain on your device until you delete them or remove app data. Deleting transactions does not delete an exported file saved elsewhere. Uninstalling the app may remove local records; keep your own exports if needed. Encryption reduces some risks but does not guarantee security on a compromised or unlocked device.

Removing local app data does not necessarily delete information held by an online messaging service. To request access, correction, or deletion of online account information, contact raincofarri@gmail.com. Applicable retention obligations and technical backups may affect when deletion is completed. Do not send passwords or payment credentials in a support request.

## Children, international processing, and your rights

The app is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age required by local law. Online service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your information. Contact raincofarri@gmail.com to make a request.

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
