# Amaveth

## Short description

Bring conversations, contacts, and groups together.

## Full description

Amaveth is a social chat app that brings your conversations, contacts, and groups together, making it easy to stay connected with friends and share everyday moments.

Keep your conversations in one place, find your contacts, and follow your group discussions. Share messages and media through the features available in the service, and choose how you receive notifications in device settings.

Features and online availability depend on the service configuration. Some features require an internet connection or device permissions. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.
